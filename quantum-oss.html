Featured contribution

Preserving classical controls in Cirq's OpenQASM output

When an operation expanded into multiple OpenQASM statements, Cirq applied its classical condition only to the first statement. I updated the exporter to apply the condition to each statement in OpenQASM 2.0 and wrap the full operation in a conditional block in OpenQASM 3.0.

I added regression tests for both formats, including a classically controlled CCZ gate, and covered multiple statements on one line and whitespace-only output. The fix was merged on 7 October 2026.
